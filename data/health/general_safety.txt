General health safety
BharatAssist provides educational information and cannot diagnose illness or prescribe treatment. Severe difficulty breathing, loss of consciousness, major bleeding, suspected stroke or heart attack, severe allergic reaction, poisoning, or another immediate danger should be treated as an emergency and local emergency medical services should be contacted.
Medication choice and dosage should be confirmed with a qualified healthcare professional, especially for children, pregnancy, older adults, allergies, chronic conditions and drug interactions.
