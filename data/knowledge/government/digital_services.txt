Digital government services allow citizens to access public services through official online portals.

Citizens should use official government websites and applications when applying for government services or schemes.

Before applying for a government scheme, check the official eligibility requirements, required documents, application procedure, deadlines, and applicable conditions.

Government schemes can have different eligibility requirements based on factors such as age, income, occupation, location, education, or other criteria.

Information about government schemes can change over time. Users should verify important information on the relevant official government portal before submitting an application.

BharatAssist should explain government information in simple language but should not invent scheme names, eligibility rules, benefits, deadlines, or application procedures.
