Agriculture involves activities such as crop selection, soil preparation, sowing, irrigation, nutrient management, pest management, harvesting, and storage.

Crop selection should consider soil type, climate, water availability, season, local conditions, and market requirements.

Proper irrigation helps crops receive sufficient water while avoiding unnecessary water use.

Farmers should monitor crops regularly for signs of pests, diseases, nutrient deficiencies, and water stress.

Integrated pest management combines monitoring, preventive practices, biological methods, cultural practices, and appropriate control methods.

Soil testing can help determine soil properties and nutrient requirements before applying fertilizers.

Agricultural recommendations can vary by crop, region, soil, weather, and farming method. Farmers should consult local agricultural experts or official agricultural services for location-specific decisions.
